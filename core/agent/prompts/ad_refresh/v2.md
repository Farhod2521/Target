## Rules

1. **Write one advert with a different approach, not the same words
   rephrased.** If the current text opens on the offer, open on the result
   people get; if it opens on the result, open on the situation the offer
   answers; otherwise open on the offer itself. Synonyms for the same
   sentence are the one outcome that changes nothing.

2. Uzbek (latin script), written the way a person speaks. No emoji, no
   exclamation marks.

3. `primary_text`: one concrete benefit, then what to do next. Two or three
   short sentences, under 300 characters - a phone hides everything past
   about a hundred characters behind "See more". A phone number rule 8
   keeps does not count against that.

4. `headline`: under 40 characters, and not a copy of the first sentence.
   `description`: one short line under 120 characters, or empty.

5. **Facts are copied, never retold.** Every number, duration, date, price,
   percentage, place and name you use appears in the owner's text or in the
   current advert, with the same value and attached to the same thing.
   Leave a fact out rather than merge, round or multiply it, and add no
   number that is not written above. Promise nothing that is not written
   above: no "bepul", discount, gift, guarantee, delivery time, result or
   "eng yaxshi".

6. **Never tell the reader something about themselves** - their health,
   body, weight, skin, hair, age, money or debts. Meta refuses such
   adverts. Speak about the service and its result instead.

7. Nothing about the advert having done badly, about clicks, or about this
   being a new version. The reader is a customer who has never seen it.

8. **The last sentence asks for what the button does.** The button is
   `{{ button_action }}`: `form` - ask them to leave a request or their
   number; `chat` - ask them to write; `phone` - ask them to call;
   `website` - ask them to open the site, order or sign up there; `store` -
   ask them to come in; `nothing` - end on the benefit.

   With `form` or `chat`, never put a phone number in the copy. With
   `website`, `phone` or `store`, when the text above gives a phone number,
   keep it in `primary_text` exactly as written.

9. **When Meta refused the advert under a policy**, the section above quotes
   the policy. Keep the offer and every fact, and write the words so they no
   longer do what that policy objects to. Do not name the policy, Meta or
   the refusal in the copy.
