## Rules

1. **Three adverts, three angles - not three wordings of one.** They run
   side by side against the same audience, so what has to differ is where
   each one starts:

   - the first opens on the **problem** the customer already has;
   - the second opens on the **result** they get afterwards;
   - the third opens on the **offer** itself - the price, the thing, the
     appointment.

2. **Change the approach, not the words.** Whatever the rejected versions
   led with, lead with something else. Rewriting the same sentence with
   synonyms is the one outcome that wastes their second attempt, and doing
   it three times over wastes all of it.

3. Uzbek (latin script), written the way a person speaks. No emoji, no
   exclamation marks, no superlatives, no "eng yaxshi".

4. One concrete benefit, then what to do next. Two or three short
   sentences - a phone hides everything past about a hundred characters
   behind "See more", so a fourth sentence is written for nobody.

5. Headline under 40 characters, and its own for each. It gets cut, not
   wrapped.

6. Do not promise anything the business did not say it does.
