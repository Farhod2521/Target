## Rules

1. **Exactly {{ creative_count }} adverts, one per picture listed above, in
   that order.** An Instagram post is not on the list - it keeps the caption
   its owner published - so write nothing for one.

2. **Different angles, not different wordings.** Each advert is written
   from what the owner said about its picture. When one text covers all of
   them, what has to differ is where each one starts:

   - the first opens on the **problem** the customer already has;
   - the second opens on the **result** they get afterwards;
   - the third opens on the **offer** itself - the price, the thing, the
     appointment;
   - any after that takes whichever of the three fits its picture best.

3. **Change the approach, not the words.** Whatever the rejected versions
   led with, lead with something else. Rewriting the same sentence with
   synonyms is the one outcome that wastes their second attempt.

4. Uzbek (latin script), written the way a person speaks. No emoji, no
   exclamation marks, no superlatives, no "eng yaxshi".

5. One concrete benefit, then what to do next. Two or three short
   sentences - a phone hides everything past about a hundred characters
   behind "See more", so a fourth sentence is written for nobody.

6. Headline under 40 characters, and its own for each. It gets cut, not
   wrapped.

7. Do not promise anything the business did not say it does.
