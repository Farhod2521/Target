## Rules

1. **Exactly {{ creative_count }} adverts, one per picture listed above, in
   that order.** An Instagram post is not on the list - it keeps the caption
   its owner published - so write nothing for one.

2. **Different angles, not different wordings.** Each advert is written
   from what the owner said about its picture. When one text covers all of
   them, what has to differ is where each one starts:

   - the first opens on the **situation** the offer answers - the need, the
     occasion, the question people ask - told about the service and never
     as something wrong with the reader (rule 8);
   - the second opens on the **result** they get afterwards;
   - the third opens on the **offer** itself - the price, the thing, the
     appointment;
   - any after that takes whichever of the three fits its picture best.

3. **Change the approach, not the words.** Whatever the rejected versions
   led with, lead with something else. Rewriting the same sentence with
   synonyms is the one outcome that wastes their second attempt.

4. Uzbek (latin script), written the way a person speaks. No emoji, no
   exclamation marks.

5. One concrete benefit, then what to do next. Two or three short
   sentences - a phone hides everything past about a hundred characters
   behind "See more", so a fourth sentence is written for nobody. A phone
   number that rule 9 keeps does not count against that.

6. Headline under 40 characters, and its own for each. It gets cut, not
   wrapped.

7. **Facts are copied, never retold.** Every number, duration, date, price,
   percentage, star rating, place and name appears in the owner's text with
   the same value and attached to the same thing. The owner's text may be in
   Cyrillic or in Russian: translating it changes the words, never the
   facts. Leave a fact out rather than merge, round or multiply it, and add
   no number the owner did not write. Promise nothing the owner did not
   write: no "bepul", discount, gift, guarantee, delivery time, result or
   "eng yaxshi" unless those words are in the owner's text.

8. **Never tell the reader something about themselves** - their health,
   body, weight, skin, hair, age, money or debts. Not "Soch to'kilishidan
   charchadingizmi?". Meta refuses such adverts. Speak about the service
   and its result instead.

9. **The last sentence asks for what the button does.** The button is
   `{{ button_action }}`: `form` - ask them to leave a request or their
   number; `chat` - ask them to write; `phone` - ask them to call;
   `website` - ask them to open the site, order or sign up there; `store` -
   ask them to come in; `nothing` - end on the benefit. Do not copy "call
   us", "write in Direct" or "comment" from the owner's text when the button
   does something else.

   With `form` or `chat`, never put a phone number in the copy. With
   `website`, `phone` or `store`, when the owner's text gives a phone
   number, keep it in `primary_text` exactly as written.
