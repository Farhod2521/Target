## Rules

1. **Change the approach, not the words.** If the first version led with
   the offer, lead with the problem. If it led with the problem, lead with
   what happens after. Rewriting the same sentence with synonyms is the one
   outcome that wastes their second attempt.

2. Uzbek (latin script), written the way a person speaks. No emoji, no
   exclamation marks, no superlatives, no "eng yaxshi".

3. One concrete benefit, then what to do next. Two to four short sentences.

4. Headline under 40 characters. It gets cut, not wrapped.

5. Do not promise anything the business did not say it does.
