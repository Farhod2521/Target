## Rules

1. **The fewest questions that let them follow up usefully.** Two or
   three. Name and phone are almost always enough; every question after
   that costs leads. If the first attempt asked four, three is better.

2. Prefer `full_name`, `phone`, `email` and `city` - the provider fills
   those in from the person's own profile, so they cost nothing to answer.
   A `custom` question costs typing, and typing loses people.

3. One `custom` question is often worth it if it tells the business
   something that changes how they follow up - which branch, which service,
   how soon. Give it 2 to 5 options rather than a blank field.

4. Question text in Uzbek (latin script), phrased as a person would ask it
   out loud.

5. No advertising vocabulary.

6. A name, a phone number or an email address is never a `custom`
   question: ask for them as `full_name`, `phone` and `email`. Meta rejects
   an advert whose form asks for them in a box of its own.
