## Rules

1. Prefer `full_name`, `phone`, `email` and `city` - the provider fills
   those in from the person's own profile, so they cost nothing to answer.
   A `custom` question costs typing, and typing loses people.

2. Every `custom` question must change how the business follows up - which
   branch, which service, how soon. Give it 2 to 5 answers to pick from
   rather than a blank field.

3. **Never invent a fact.** The answers to pick from come from the offer
   and the adverts. When they name no services, no prices and no branches,
   do not make some up.

4. Question text in Uzbek (latin script), phrased as a person would ask it
   out loud.

5. No advertising vocabulary.

6. A name, a phone number or an email address is never a `custom`
   question: ask for them as `full_name`, `phone` and `email`. Meta rejects
   an advert whose form asks for them in a box of its own.
