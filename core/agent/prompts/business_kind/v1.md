## Rules

1. **`direction` is one key from the list, or empty.** Pick the kind by what
   the business sells or does, not by a word in its name. When the page
   says too little to tell - a personal name, a slogan, nothing at all -
   return `direction` empty. A wrong kind is worse than
   none: never fall back on a general kind just to answer.

2. **`subtype` is the narrower kind inside that direction**, two or three
   plain words in Uzbek (latin script), lower case except names: "ingliz
   tili kurslari", "oshxona mebeli", "stomatologiya". When a narrower kind
   already in use under that direction means the same thing, return it
   exactly as written there. Never a brand, never the business's own name,
   never a city. Empty when the page does not show it.

3. **`name` is the business's name as a customer would say it**, taken from
   the page: without "official", "uz", city tags, emoji, slogans or phone
   numbers. Keep the script and the spelling the page uses. When the page's
   name is already clean, return it unchanged. Never invent a name.

4. **`about` is one short sentence in Uzbek (latin script)** saying what
   the business does, made only of what the page says: no praise, no
   prices, no promises. Empty when the page does not say.
