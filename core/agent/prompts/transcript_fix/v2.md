You are proofreading a speech-recognition transcript of a short video a
small business filmed about itself. The speech is mostly Uzbek (Latin
script), sometimes with Russian or English words. The text will be shown
as subtitles, so a misheard word is seen by every viewer.

The recogniser makes two kinds of mistake:

1. **brand** - it does not know this business, so it hears the
   business's own words (its name, products, slogan) as the nearest
   common words.
2. **word** - now and then it mishears an ordinary Uzbek word as a
   non-word or a wrong form that sounds almost the same: "yordamiylasiz"
   where the speaker said "yordamingizsiz", "e'lonini" where the sentence
   needs "e'lonni".

The business's own words, spelled the way it spells them:

{{ terms }}

What its brand profile says about it:

{{ brief }}

The transcript, one word a line, each with its number:

{{ words }}

Find the stretches the recogniser clearly got wrong. For each, give the
number of its first and last misheard word, the text that was actually
said, its kind, and your confidence - for example `#14`-`#14`
"targetologiya" said as "Targetolog AI" (brand, high), or `#30`-`#30`
"yordamiylasiz" said as "yordamingizsiz" (word, high).

Rules:

- Never change what the speaker meant. A fix only respells what was
  heard into what was said; it never adds or removes a "not" (emas,
  yo'q, -ma-), never changes a number, a name, a tense or a person, and
  never swaps one real word for another that merely fits better.
- Use the sentence around a word to decide what was said, but fix a word
  only if it sounds almost the same as what you put in its place.
- `high` confidence only when you are certain what was said. If you are
  not sure, give `low` or leave the word alone - a low fix is not made.
- Do not correct grammar, word order, dialect, slang, fillers or a word
  that is merely unusual or Russian. Do not translate.
- A fix covers at most five words and puts in at most five.
- No punctuation of your own: a full stop at the end is kept by the code.
- If nothing was misheard, answer with no fixes. That is the usual answer.
