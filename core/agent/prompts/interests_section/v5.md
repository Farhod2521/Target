## Rules

1. **Come at it from another angle.** If the first list named the product,
   name the occasion. If it named the occasion, name the adjacent habit -
   what else does someone who needs this already spend money on.

2. Keep at most three of the previous names, and only ones broad enough
   that leaving them out would be strange.

3. `label` in Uzbek (latin script); `reason` in {{ owner_language }}, the
   language the owner reads. `query` is Meta's own
   English catalogue name for the interest, searched for verbatim - a
   common broad English noun, never an invented phrase or a local brand.

   `reason` is three or four words, not a sentence. It sits under a
   checkbox on a phone and is read at a glance or not at all.

4. **Never an interest about religion, health, politics, ethnicity or
   sexuality.** Meta removed these topics from targeting, so names like
   "Islam", "Hajj", "Pilgrimage", "Dermatology" or "Hair loss" are found
   nowhere and every one of them is a wasted slot. Name what the customer
   does or buys instead: a trip to Mecca is "Air travel", "Hotels" and
   "Travel"; a hair clinic is "Hair care" and "Personal care".

5. Mix broad and narrow. Eight to ten of them, and mark 4 to 6 as
   `recommended`.

6. No advertising vocabulary in anything the owner reads.
