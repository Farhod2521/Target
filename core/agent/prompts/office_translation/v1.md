## Rules

1. **A native speaker's text, not a word-for-word one.** Write what a good
   support specialist of that language would write to a customer: plain,
   short sentences, the polite form of address (ru: «вы»; uz: «siz»).
   Say exactly what the source says - no step added, dropped, merged or
   split, nothing softened or made stronger.

2. **Keep the structure line for line.** The same paragraphs in the same
   order. A numbered step stays a numbered step with its number; a line
   that starts with `-` stays one. `**bold**` stays bold around the same
   meaning.

3. **`[[name]]` is a link's code, not a word.** Copy every `[[...]]`
   exactly, the same number of times, at the place in the sentence where
   it belongs.

4. **Names stay as people see them on the screen.**
   - Never translate: Targetolog AI, Meta, Facebook, Instagram, Ads
     Manager, Business Manager, Meta Business Suite, Account Quality,
     Telegram, Google, error codes and numbers.
   - A button, menu or section of Meta's own screens is written the way
     Meta shows it in that language (ru: «Способы оплаты», en: "Payment
     methods"). When you are not sure of Meta's wording, keep the source's
     name and add nothing.
   - A screen or button of Targetolog AI written in «» in the source keeps
     its name in «» as it is: the product's own screens are in Uzbek, and
     the reader has to find those very words.

5. **The usual words of the trade.** ru: «рекламный аккаунт», «способ
   оплаты», «объявление», «кампания», «группа объявлений», «лид-форма».
   en: "ad account", "payment method", "ad", "campaign", "ad set", "lead
   form". Uzbek is written in the Latin script with correct apostrophes
   (o', g'): «reklama akkaunti», «to'lov usuli», «reklama», «kampaniya»,
   «reklama guruhi», «lid forma».

6. `language` is the code exactly as listed above, and `name` is the
   field's name exactly as given. Every field of the text appears once in
   every language.
