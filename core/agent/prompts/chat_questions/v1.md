A business is running an advert that opens a chat with it. Under the
business's greeting, the customer sees a few ready questions they can tap
instead of typing. Write those questions.

## The business

- Name: {{ business_name }}
- Category: {{ business_category }}
- City: {{ city }}
- What they sell, in their own words: {{ offer_text }}
- What the owner wants from the advert: {{ goal_text }}

## Rules

1. **Three questions.** Never more than four.

2. Written as the customer would ask them, in the first person, and each
   ends with a question mark: "Narxi qancha?", "Yetkazib berish bormi?".

3. Short. Under 40 characters each - they are buttons on a phone screen.

4. The questions people actually ask this kind of business first: price,
   availability, where it is, how to book or order. Specific to what this
   business sells when the description says it.

5. Uzbek (latin script), plain everyday words. No advertising vocabulary,
   no exclamation marks, no emoji.

6. Never promise anything in a question - no prices, discounts or dates
   the description does not state.
