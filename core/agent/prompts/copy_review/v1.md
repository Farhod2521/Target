## Your answer

One JSON object with one key, `adverts`: exactly {{ creative_count }} items,
in the order above. Each item has `problems`, then `primary_text`,
`headline` and `description`.

- When an advert has no problem, `problems` is an empty list and the three
  fields are copied back unchanged, character for character.
- When it has any, list each one and return the advert rewritten so that
  none remains. Change only what the problem needs: keep the angle, the
  length and everything that was already right.

## The problems

Each has a `code` and a `quote` - the wrong words, copied exactly from the
advert.

- `changed_fact` - a number, duration, date, price, percentage, star
  rating, place or name that is not in the owner's text, or is attached to
  a different thing than it is there. "5 kun Madinada, 5 kun Makkada"
  written as "10 kun Madina, 10 kun Makka" is this, even when 10 appears
  somewhere else in the owner's text. The owner may have written in Cyrillic
  or in Russian: compare the meaning, not the script.
- `invented_promise` - anything the business is said to offer or achieve
  that the owner did not write: a free consultation, a discount, a gift, a
  guarantee, a delivery time, "butunlay yo'q qiladi", "bir necha kunda",
  "eng yaxshi".
- `personal_attribute` - a sentence that says or asks something about the
  reader's own health, body, weight, skin, hair, age, money or debts:
  "Soch to'kilishidan charchadingizmi?". Rewrite it about the service or
  its result.
- `action_mismatch` - the copy asks for something the button does not do.
  `form`: a form opens, so the copy asks them to leave a request or their
  number - not to call, write, comment, open a site or order. `chat`: a
  conversation opens, ask them to write. `phone`: ask them to call.
  `website`: ask them to open the site, order or sign up there. `store`: ask
  them to come in. `nothing`: no request at all is fine.
- `contact_rule` - with `form` or `chat`, the copy contains a phone number:
  remove it. With `website`, `phone` or `store`, the owner's text gives a
  phone number and `primary_text` does not carry it: add it exactly as the
  owner wrote it.

## Rules for a rewrite

1. Uzbek, Latin script, written the way a person speaks. No emoji, no
   exclamation marks.
2. `primary_text` at most 300 characters, `headline` at most 40,
   `description` at most 120.
3. Never bring in a new fact while fixing an old one. When you are not sure
   something is in the owner's text, leave it out.
4. Never mention Meta, targeting or any advertising term.
