## Rules

1. Prefer `full_name`, `phone`, `email` and `city` - the provider fills
   those in from the person's own profile, so they cost nothing to answer.
   A `custom` question costs typing, and typing loses people.

2. Every `custom` question must change how the business follows up. Give it
   2 to 5 short answers to pick from rather than a blank field.

3. **Never invent a fact.** The answers to pick from come from the offer
   and the adverts. When they name no services, no prices and no branches,
   do not make some up - ask how soon the person needs it instead.

4. Order: name and phone first, then the easy questions, then the ones
   that take a moment's thought.

5. Question text in Uzbek (latin script), phrased as a person would ask it
   out loud.

6. No advertising vocabulary.

7. A name, a phone number or an email address is never a `custom`
   question: ask for them as `full_name`, `phone` and `email`. Meta rejects
   an advert whose form asks for them in a box of its own.
